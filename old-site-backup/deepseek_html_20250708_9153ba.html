About Easily-PM Tablets

Easily-PM Tablets are a combination of Pregabalin, Epalrestat and Methylcobalamin specifically formulated for the management of diabetic neuropathic pain. This WHO-GMP certified medication helps in:

  • Reducing neuropathic pain associated with diabetes
  • Improving nerve conduction
  • Reducing tingling and numbness in extremities
  • Managing chronic pain conditions

How Easily-PM Tablets Work

Easily-PM combines three active ingredients that work synergistically:

Pregabalin: Binds to calcium channels in the central nervous system, reducing pain signals.

Epalrestat: Inhibits aldose reductase, preventing nerve damage caused by high blood sugar.

Methylcobalamin: Repairs damaged nerves and improves nerve function.

Directions for Use

Take one tablet twice daily or as directed by your physician. Swallow whole with water, preferably after meals.

Safety Information

  • Store below 30°C in a dry place
  • Keep out of reach of children
  • Not recommended during pregnancy
  • May cause dizziness - avoid driving if affected
