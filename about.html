About Invision Health Care

Trusted Pharmaceutical Solutions Since 2010

Invision Health Care is a leading WHO-GMP certified pharmaceutical company established in 2010 and headquartered in New Delhi. We specialize in manufacturing high-quality medicines across multiple therapeutic categories including neuropathic pain management, diabetes care, gastrointestinal health, and bone care treatments.

Our Commitment to Quality

Every product from Invision Health Care, from our flagship Easily-PM (Pregabalin + Epalrestat + Methylcobalamin) for neuropathic pain to Sayomax (Sitagliptin + Metformin) for diabetes management, undergoes rigorous testing to ensure safety, efficacy, and compliance with international quality standards.

Our Product Philosophy

We combine scientific innovation with affordability to deliver effective healthcare solutions. Our portfolio includes both prescription medications like Doronec-150 (Ibandronic Acid) for osteoporosis and OTC products such as Polycool Eye Drops for dry eye relief.

Neuropathic Pain Relief

Bone & Nutrition
